# Luna assignment patterns

Use one lens only when its uncertainty is material and independent. Before
using any template, add the explicit Luna/max spawn pins, read-only leaf
boundary, and complete bounded evidence-packet contract from `SKILL.md` so the
assignment is self-contained. Do not turn a lens into solution ownership.

## `luna_preflight_*` — repository and dependency map

Map `[bounded phase/surface]` for the pending decision `[decision]`. Inventory
only relevant files, dependencies, boundaries, and likely evidence volume.
Stop when Sol can route the phase confidently or one material unknown remains.

## `luna_contract_*` — contract and consumer discovery

Trace `[interface/behavior]` through its authoritative contract, producers,
consumers, nearby working pattern, and directly relevant tests within
`[bounded surface]`. Identify compatibility constraints and contract drift.

## `luna_research_*` — external-source reconciliation

Reconcile `[question]` across `[bounded primary sources]` for `[decision]`.
Compare versions, dates, authority, and contradictions; distinguish documented
behavior from inference and stop when the sources are decision-ready.

## `luna_falsify_*` — competing-hypothesis test

Test leading hypothesis `[A]` against strongest competitor `[B]` using
`[bounded read-only surface]`. Seek counterevidence and run or propose the one
probe most likely to discriminate between them.

## `luna_failure_*` — failure cluster and differential

Compare expected `[expected]` with observed `[observed]` across `[exact logs,
runs, or artifacts]`. Cluster failures and warnings, test failed hypothesis
`[hypothesis]`, identify changed versus unchanged evidence, and assess the
strongest competing explanation.

## `luna_verify_*` — requirements-to-tests coverage

Map `[requirements]` to `[tests/checks/artifacts]`. Identify which observable
behaviors are covered, missing, contradictory, or only inferred. Do not run
mutation-capable checks or make the final verification judgment.

## `luna_provenance_*` — claim and residual-unknown audit

Audit `[candidate claims]` against `[bounded sources/artifacts]`. Cite decisive
provenance, flag unsupported or overstated claims, reconcile contradictions,
and surface residual unknowns that could change the final synthesis.
